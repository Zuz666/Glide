Addon and game
Where WoW is installed, and the Glide addon that sends the game to the coach.

    

      

      

        
World of Warcraft folder

        
The client folder for the version you play, such as …\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_. The addon goes in its Interface\AddOns; the coach reads your recordings from WTF and the quest cache from Cache\WDB.

        

          

        

        

      

      

        
Glide addon

        

        

          

        

        
After installing or updating, restart WoW completely; /reload doesn't load new addon files. Your addon settings and recordings are kept: they live in WTF\…\SavedVariables.

      

      

        
Add a RestedXP guide

        
Choose the guide file you downloaded from RestedXP, or paste its string, and you don't have to stop playing to import it. Glide decrypts it now: the coach learns the route right away, and at your next login the Glide addon loads the decrypted guides into RestedXP, so you never open its import window. It works for the Battle.net account that plays RestedXP on this PC.
